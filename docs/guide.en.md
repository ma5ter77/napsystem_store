# NAP Store — quick guide

**Authors.** Sign up and tick “I'm an author” → “New app” → “Audio & converter”: upload WAV/FLAC/MP3/M4A (up to 1 GB, 120 min, mono or stereo) → pick a profile → “Convert”. Listen to the source and the result and check the QA report (carrier frequencies, true peak, LUFS). In “Details” fill in RU/EN texts and equipment requirements, and confirm your rights. “Program” builds sessions from finished tracks. “Price”: one-time, subscription or free. “Versions & publishing”: submit for review, then “Publish” once approved. Changes go into a new version. Earnings: 70% to you, 30% to the platform (see “Earnings”). SDK keys are for servers only.

**Buyers.** Pick an app, listen to the fragment, buy or subscribe (test payment in demo). In “Library” press “Play”, choose a device (headphones in this browser or the chair simulator), do the L/R check, the optional “before” questionnaire, the session and the “after” questionnaire. Scores are self-ratings, not medical measures. “Find equipment” on an app page shows kits and missing parts; mark what you already own.

**Hardware.** The cart is split into one order per seller. In each order enter the address (fields depend on the country), calculate shipping, pick a rate and pay. For a chair, request a shipping quote from the supplier first. Delivery times are estimates. Under DAP, import duties are paid by the recipient.

**Suppliers.** “Supplier cabinet” → order → “Accept for fulfillment” → packages → label (or your own carrier with a tracking number) → “Handed to carrier” → delivery events. Returns: approve → receive → inspect (stock is replenished only after inspection) → money refund as a separate step.

**Moderators/admins.** Moderation: listen, check QA and the manifest, decide with a comment. Administration: roles, DSP profiles (new version), finance and payouts, audit, queues.
